## 欢迎！这是我的日记
### 互动
这是我的日记，不是代码，仅仅是为了有趣而已！
欢迎大家通过PR请求纠正日记中的客观事实错误，或者留下"读者留言"。也可以提issues来提问相关话题、分享自己的经历、做出感慨、表达自己的想法……
也欢迎大家fork这个仓库，一起打开脑洞改写：如果当时是这样的，故事会怎么继续。
### 更新
至于更新……随缘吧！只要我没有将这个存储库标记为公共存档，它就没有停止更新，只不过更新得很慢而已。
### 🏠 互动公约（互动前请瞄一眼）
为了让大家玩得开心，也为了守住底线，咱们约法三章：
- 礼貌第一：可以争论观点，但**别搞人身攻击、地域歧视或网络霸凌**。
- 守法为主：请勿发布违反法律法规及 GitHub 服务条款的内容（包括但不限于黄赌毒、暴恐、谣言、政治敏感话题）。
- 责任自负：您留下的每一句话，由您自己负责。如果发现违规内容，我会直接删除或关闭评论，并视情况向平台举报。
🗒免责小贴士：**日记内容仅代表我个人当下的心情，不构成任何建议。大家的留言仅代表留言者个人，与我无关（但违规的我依然会删哦）**。

## Welcome! This is my diary
### Interaction
This is my diary, not code, just for fun!
Everyone is welcome to correct objective factual errors in the diary via pull requests, or leave "reader comments". You can also open issues to ask related questions, share your own experiences, make remarks, express your thoughts…
You are also welcome to fork this repository and unleash your imagination to rewrite: if things had been different at that time, how would the story continue?
### Updates
As for updates… it depends on fate! As long as I don't mark this repository as public archive, it hasn't stopped updating — it just updates very slowly.
### 🏠 Interaction Guidelines (please take a glance before interacting)
To make sure everyone has a good time and to keep things within bounds, here are a few ground rules:
- **Politeness first**: You may debate opinions, but **no personal attacks, regional discrimination, or cyberbullying**.
- **Law-abiding primarily**: Please do not post content that violates laws, regulations, or GitHub's Terms of Service (including but not limited to pornography, gambling, violence, terrorism, rumors, or politically sensitive topics).
- **Self-responsibility**: Every word you leave is your own responsibility. If I find any rule‑breaking content, I will delete it or close the comment directly, and report it to the platform as appropriate.
🗒 **Disclaimer**: **The diary content only represents my current personal feelings and does not constitute any advice. Comments from others represent only the commenters themselves and have nothing to do with me (but I will still delete those that violate the rules).**
